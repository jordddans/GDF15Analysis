# Figure 3. Calibration of the fitted model by quintile of predicted probability.

The 166 patients of the PVOD versus IPAH + HPAH model, ordered by predicted
probability and split into fifths. Open diamonds are the mean predicted probability within
each fifth; filled circles are the observed proportion with PVOD, with exact
(Clopper–Pearson) 95% intervals. Counts below each fifth are PVOD cases over patients;
the GDF15 range spanned by each fifth is given on the axis. Observed proportions:
Q1 0/34, Q2 0/33, Q3 1/33, Q4 4/33, Q5 21/33.
Predicted probabilities are those of the fitted model and therefore apply at the sampling
prevalence of this cohort (15.7%); the prevalence-independent
form of the model is the likelihood-ratio curve in Figure 1.

Internal validation is a bootstrap optimism correction (3,991 replicates,
seed 1), reported apparent against optimism-corrected. C statistic:
0.9560 apparent, 0.9562 corrected
(optimism -0.0001). Calibration slope:
1.0000 apparent, 0.9676
corrected (optimism +0.0324). The apparent slope is 1 by
construction, so the corrected value is what the resampling has to say about it.

Source data: Prabhakar et al., J Clin Invest 2026;136(2):e199159, Supporting data values, sheet GDF15-1.
