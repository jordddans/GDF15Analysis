# Figure 1. Plasma GDF15 reported as a threshold and as continuous evidence.

(A) Observed concentrations in 166 patients: 26 PVOD,
98 IPAH and 42 HPAH. Vertical bars mark medians.
(B) Ordinary logistic regression on log10 GDF15, with study sampling log-odds
subtracted to give a likelihood ratio. The pointwise 95% band uses 4,000
requested stratified resamples (seed 20260907): 3,991 finite fits and
9 separated resamples excluded. The band narrows in the tail after these exclusions.
The empirical cutoff rule assigns LR 0.20 below
1,658 pg/mL and 28.3 at or above it.
Both observed support boundaries are marked: 861 pg/mL
(lowest PVOD) and 1,809.5 pg/mL (highest comparator).
Beyond either boundary the curve is broken and the band is lighter.
(C) Every plotted patient is observed in the published source. Filled points show
model-derived post-test probability and open points show the cutoff-rule probability,
both at 10% pre-test probability; joining lines show their difference. The adjacent
observed IPAH and PVOD patients from Table 1 remain the centerpiece. Shapes distinguish
PVOD from comparators. No hypothetical patients are added.

Nine of 4,000 stratified bootstrap resamples produced complete separation, for which the maximum likelihood estimate is not finite. Percentile intervals for the Figure 1 band were computed from the 3,991 resamples with finite fits. Excluding the nine leaves the upper edge effectively unchanged at and below the published cutoff and narrows it within the extrapolated tail, by approximately 2% at 2,156 pg/mL and 17% at 5,000 pg/mL.

Source: Prabhakar et al., J Clin Invest 2026;136(2):e199159, published supporting
workbook Fig_1a (equivalent cleaned alias GDF15-1).
