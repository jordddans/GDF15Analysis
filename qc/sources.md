## A8: 2026 diagnostic score — VERIFIED

Andruska AM, Cruz-Utrilla A, Nossent EJ, et al. Identification of pulmonary arterial hypertension patients with venous or capillary involvement. *American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine*. 2026;212(9):2199–2210. Published online 11 June 2026. DOI [10.1093/ajrccm/aamag289](https://academic.oup.com/ajrccm/article/212/9/2199/8706424). [PMID 42275164](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42275164/).

Local origin of the citation lead was also found in Giorgio's historical note, retained privately at `analysis-v1.0-published-data:archive/Professor notes.pages`: it names the Stanford, Madrid and Amsterdam score published in AJRCCM in June and its dichotomization limitation. The note is corroborated by the published paper, not used as sole evidence.

The Discussion paragraph beginning “The approach for developing…” explicitly lists “information loss from dichotomization at thresholds” among the statistical limitations. The adjoining discussion explains that aggregate published cohort statistics constrained development and that conventional regression/Bayesian/machine-learning approaches would impose unverifiable assumptions about joint distributions. Thus the narrow manuscript claim about the score's acknowledged dichotomization limitation is supported. The separate claim that validation data are unavailable to this project is not established by the citation alone and remains an audit issue.
