## Figure 1

**Plasma GDF15 separates PVOD from other pulmonary hypertension only at the extremes.**

Raw plasma GDF15 (pg/mL) for 322 individuals: control (n = 68), Other PH (n = 52), HPAH (n = 42), COPD (n = 36), IPAH (n = 98), PVOD (n = 26). Each point is one patient, plotted as a within-cohort swarm; the black rule across each swarm is that cohort's median. Cohort is encoded redundantly by both colour and marker shape, and hues were assigned so that the cohorts the figure exists to separate stay distinct after grayscale conversion and under simulated protan/deutan colour-vision deficiency. Horizontal lines mark the two empirical separation boundaries: 860.5 pg/mL, below which no PVOD patient falls, and 1,809.5 pg/mL, above which only PVOD patients fall. **Both lines are raw order statistics of these observed data — respectively the minimum of the PVOD cohort and the maximum of the pooled non-PVOD cohorts — not fitted or optimised thresholds; no confidence interval is shown and none is implied.** The shaded band between them contains 85 patients (PVOD 6, IPAH 41, COPD 15, HPAH 10, Other PH 13) whose GDF15 value alone does not resolve their diagnosis. GDF15 differs across cohorts (Kruskal–Wallis H = 149.2, df = 5, P = 2.0 × 10⁻³⁰); PVOD exceeds every other cohort pairwise (Mann–Whitney U, Holm-adjusted P ≤ 1.7 × 10⁻⁹ for all 5 comparisons). Cohort medians (IQR), pg/mL: Ctrl 255.7 (157.3–339.4); Other PH 548.1 (302.9–805.3); HPAH 606.1 (398.6–716.1); COPD 620.4 (474.9–1,087.1); IPAH 777.0 (484.9–1,115.0); PVOD 3,170.7 (2,151.2–3,634.1). *y* axis is log₁₀-scaled (linear variant: *y* axis is linear). Source data: Prabhakar et al., *J Clin Invest* 2026;136(2):e199159, Supplementary Source Data, sheet `Fig_1a` (panel Fig. 1a).

## Supplementary Figure S1

**As Figure 1, with every patient individually labelled by diagnosis.**

Identical data and boundaries to Figure 1 (n = 322; group n as above), with all 322 patients labelled individually. Patients are dealt by rank into evenly spaced columns purely to make room for the labels; horizontal position carries no meaning, and every point is drawn at its true value on the shared vertical axis. Where a label has been displaced vertically to avoid overlapping its neighbour, a leader line connects it to its point. Lines as in Figure 1 — raw order statistics of the observed data, no interval implied. Note the single IPAH patient at 1,809.5 pg/mL lying exactly on the upper boundary: that one patient is what prevents a cleaner threshold. Per-point values underlying every figure are tabulated in `gdf15_ladder_points.csv`. Source data: Prabhakar et al., *J Clin Invest* 2026;136(2):e199159, Supplementary Source Data, sheet `Fig_1a` (panel Fig. 1a).
