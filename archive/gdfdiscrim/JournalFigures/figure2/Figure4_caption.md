**Figure 4 | Plasma GDF15 in all 322 individuals, shown as an unstructured scatter against the two separation boundaries.**

Raw plasma GDF15 (pg/mL) for 322 individuals: control (n = 68), Other PH (n = 52), HPAH (n = 42), COPD (n = 36), IPAH (n = 98), PVOD (n = 26). Each point is one patient. Horizontal position is random jitter applied solely to separate overlapping points; it carries no information about the patient, and the jitter is generated from a fixed seed so the figure is reproducible. No density estimate, kernel or distributional envelope is fitted or drawn — the horizontal extent of the cloud is uniform by construction and must not be read as a distribution shape. Cohort is encoded redundantly by both colour and marker shape, and hues were assigned so that the cohorts the figure exists to separate stay distinct after grayscale conversion and under simulated protan/deutan colour-vision deficiency. Horizontal lines mark the two empirical separation boundaries: 860.5 pg/mL, below which no PVOD patient falls, and 1,809.5 pg/mL, above which only PVOD patients fall. Both lines are raw order statistics of these observed data — respectively the minimum of the PVOD cohort and the maximum of the pooled non-PVOD cohorts — not fitted or optimised thresholds; no confidence interval is shown and none is implied. The shaded band between them contains 85 patients (PVOD 6, IPAH 41, COPD 15, HPAH 10, Other PH 13) whose GDF15 value alone does not resolve their diagnosis. GDF15 differs across cohorts (Kruskal–Wallis H = 149.2, df = 5, P = 2.0 × 10⁻³⁰); PVOD exceeds every other cohort pairwise (Mann–Whitney U, Holm-adjusted P ≤ 1.7 × 10⁻⁹ for all 5 comparisons). Cohort medians (IQR), pg/mL: Ctrl 255.7 (157.3–339.4); Other PH 548.1 (302.9–805.3); HPAH 606.1 (398.6–716.1); COPD 620.4 (474.9–1,087.1); IPAH 777.0 (484.9–1,115.0); PVOD 3,170.7 (2,151.2–3,634.1). y axis is linear. Source data: Prabhakar et al., J Clin Invest 2026;136(2):e199159, Supplementary Source Data, sheet Fig_1a (panel Fig. 1a).
